# Cancellation Policy

Policy ID: POL-CANCEL-001

Version: 1.0

Category: Cancellation

## Purpose

This policy defines when an order may be cancelled.

## Eligible Cancellation

An order may be cancelled when:

- the order exists
- the customer owns the order
- the order has not yet been shipped
- payment and order records are consistent

## Ineligible Cancellation

An order cannot be cancelled when:

- shipment is already marked as shipped or delivered
- the order has already been cancelled
- the order cannot be verified
- the customer does not own the order

## After Shipment

If the order has already shipped, SupportCommander must not cancel it.

Instead, the system may:

- explain that cancellation is no longer available
- provide return or refund guidance
- escalate when policy conditions are unclear

## Human Approval

Human approval is required when:

- shipment status is inconsistent
- payment status conflicts with order status
- a high-value order exceeds 500 USD
- account risk level is medium or higher

## Escalation

Escalate when:

- order ownership cannot be verified
- conflicting shipment data exists
- suspicious account activity is detected
