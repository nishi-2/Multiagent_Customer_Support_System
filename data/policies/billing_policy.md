# Billing Policy

Policy ID: POL-BILLING-001

Version: 1.0

Category: Billing

## Purpose

This policy covers billing questions and payment discrepancies.

## Billing Inquiry

SupportCommander may provide billing information when:

- the customer identity can be linked to the order
- the payment record exists
- the amount can be verified

## Payment Status

A payment may be classified as:

- paid
- pending
- failed
- refunded
- partially_refunded

## Duplicate Charge

A suspected duplicate charge requires verification against payment records.

SupportCommander must not automatically issue a refund solely because a customer reports a duplicate charge.

## Amount Discrepancy

If the customer reports a charged amount different from the order amount:

- compare payment and order values
- do not modify the transaction automatically
- escalate when the values conflict

## High-Risk Billing Cases

Human review is required when:

- multiple payments exist unexpectedly
- payment records conflict
- account risk is medium or higher
- the customer disputes ownership of the transaction
- suspected fraud exists

## Escalation

Escalate when financial records cannot be reconciled.
