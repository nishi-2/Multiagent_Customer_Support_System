# Refund Policy

Policy ID: POL-REFUND-001

Version: 1.0

Category: Refund

## Purpose

This policy defines when SupportCommander may recommend or execute a refund.

## Eligibility

A refund may be considered when:

- the customer has a valid completed payment
- the order exists and belongs to the customer
- the product was purchased successfully
- the customer provides a valid reason for the refund
- no completed refund already exists for the same order

## Refund Window

A standard refund is allowed within 30 days of the purchase date.

Requests submitted after 30 days require human review.

## Refund Amount

The refund amount must not exceed the original paid amount.

Partial refunds may be considered when only part of the purchase is affected.

## Autonomous Refund Threshold

SupportCommander may automatically approve a refund only when:

- the customer account is active
- the risk level is low
- the refund amount is less than or equal to 200 USD
- the request is within the standard refund window
- there is no previous completed refund for the same order
- there are no suspicious signals

## Human Approval

Human approval is required when:

- the refund amount is greater than 200 USD
- the request is outside the 30-day refund window
- the account is restricted
- the risk level is medium or higher
- there are conflicting transaction records
- the customer has multiple recent refund requests

## Refund Rejection

A refund should not be executed when:

- payment was never completed
- the requested amount exceeds the original payment amount
- the order does not belong to the customer
- the order was already fully refunded
- fraud or abuse is strongly suspected

## Escalation

The case must be escalated when:

- policy conditions conflict
- identity or ownership cannot be verified
- fraud risk is high
- transaction data is inconsistent
