# Account Risk Policy

Policy ID: POL-RISK-001

Version: 1.0

Category: Risk

## Purpose

This policy defines how account and transaction risk should affect autonomous actions.

## Low Risk

Low-risk customers may be eligible for automated actions when all other policy conditions are satisfied.

## Medium Risk

Medium-risk cases require additional review.

Transactional actions such as refunds, replacements, or cancellations should require human approval.

## High Risk

High-risk accounts must not receive autonomous transactional actions.

The case must be escalated.

## Restricted Accounts

If the account status is restricted:

- do not automatically issue refunds
- do not automatically issue replacements
- do not automatically cancel financial transactions
- require human review

## Suspicious Signals

Examples include:

- multiple recent refund requests
- inconsistent customer and order ownership
- conflicting payment records
- unusual transaction patterns
- repeated high-value claims

## Escalation

High-risk or strongly suspicious cases must be escalated.
