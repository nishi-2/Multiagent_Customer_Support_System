# Replacement Policy

Policy ID: POL-REPLACE-001

Version: 1.0

Category: Replacement

## Purpose

This policy defines when a product replacement may be offered.

## Eligible Replacement

A replacement may be considered when:

- the product is defective
- the product arrived damaged
- troubleshooting has failed
- the order and customer can be verified
- the product is still within the supported replacement window

## Replacement Window

Standard replacement eligibility is 30 days from purchase.

Requests outside the standard replacement window require human review.

## Automatic Replacement

SupportCommander may automatically approve a replacement when:

- the customer account is active
- account risk is low
- the product value is less than or equal to 300 USD
- no previous completed replacement exists for the same order
- the request is within 30 days of purchase

## Human Approval

Human approval is required when:

- the product value exceeds 300 USD
- a replacement was previously issued
- the customer risk level is medium or high
- the replacement window has expired
- transaction records conflict

## Rejection

A replacement should not be automatically issued when:

- the customer cannot verify the order
- the product is outside the replacement window
- there is evidence of abuse or fraud
- a completed replacement already exists

## Escalation

Escalate when eligibility cannot be determined safely.
